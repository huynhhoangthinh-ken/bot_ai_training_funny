BRINGING HAUTE COUTURE TO VILLA LIVING

From the outset, the vision of THE RIVUS was to design a sophisticated and exclusive villa development that it
is attractive, modern and distinguished. Since then, through a long interactive process of exchanging ideas with
the clicnt, appreciating the potential of this unique site and working alongside distinguished fellow designers

including the ELIE SAAB design team and Belt Collins Landscape Architects, THE RIVUS have achieved so
much more.

The essence of Vietnamese high culture and tradition

This private estate concept connects to the essence of Vietnamese high culture and tradition through its
architecture, spatial planning and landscape design. The materials used and the physical form of the buildings
convey an exclusive village impression, which is reinforced through well-considered planning which also
responds to traditional values. There is a careful use of natural stone claddings and screens, expressed balcony
surrounds and well-proportioned and thoughtfully positioned windows and doors. All are designed to optimize
natural lighting and spatial relationships to light and ventilation to take advantage of cooling breezes, allowing
families to live passively within their environment as well as protecting the interiors from heavy seasonal rains.

The best of modern design systems

The architectural design process continues and connects each stage, from the aesthetics of the residential exteriors
to the planning of the private facilities and inclusions within the residences themselves. The villa architecture
embodies the best of modern design standards, advanced technologies and environmental systems. The
connections extend from the ways the villas are designed to overlook the gardens and views beyond, to the ways
the central staircases interconnect the living spaces at different levels, to finer details such as the ways the external
materials, textures and finishes are layered and joined together with the interior spaces to soothe the transition
process from the external world to the private inner realm.

Design features inspired by the internationally famous fashion and interior designer ELIE SAAB

As well as offering exceptionally luxurious interior designs, ELIE SAAB’s designer’s eye has informed the
overall look and aesthetic appeal of the villas, influencing the colours, material selections, scale, form and layering
of the facades as well as the external garden areas and landscape facilities.
