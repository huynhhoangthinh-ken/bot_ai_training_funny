Participation Registration

Participation Registration is construed that when the customer has completed the

Masterise Agents Agency Company Limited (“Participation Registration’).

Successful Registration

nhượng hợp đồng mua bán đối với Sản Phẩm bằng việc hoàn tất ký Bản đăng ký
nguyện vọng, Xác nhận đăng ky và đã thanh toán xong Tiền đặt trước (“Bang Ký Thanh
Công )).

Successful Registration is construed that when the customers have completed the
aspiration registration to choose to receive the transfer of the sale and purchase
contract for the Product with the completion of signing the Aspiration Declaration
Form, the Registration Confirmation and completion of payment of the Advance
Amount (the “Successful Registration’).
