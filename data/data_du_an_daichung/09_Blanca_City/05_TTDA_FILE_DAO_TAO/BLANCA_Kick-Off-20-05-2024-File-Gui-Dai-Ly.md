###### THE WHALE IN VUNG TAU 

###### _‘MR. NAM HAI’_ 

Mr. Nam Hai is revered as the protector of fishermen and sailors along the southern coastal regions of Vietnam. Worshipped as a sea god, he is believed to have the power to calm storms, protect sailors and ensure the safety of those who venture into the sea. 

Nam Hai is often depicted as an elderly man with a long beard, or sometimes as a whale, symbolizing the whale’s role as a guardian of the ocean. 

###### SAVING _‘THE GIA LONG KING’_ WHEN THE SHIPWRECKED 

Gia Long was shipwrecked while escaping from his enemies. As the ship sank and the situation became desperate, a whale appeared and helped save him by guiding the vessel to safety. This allowed Gia Long to later reunite the country and bring peace. 

The whale was believed to have been sent to protect and guide the king, giving him a second chance to restore the Nguyen dynasty. This event is seen as a key moment in Gia Long’s rise to power and his eventual victory over his adversaries.
